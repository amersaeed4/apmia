***Ref No.: APMIA/HO/2019/2000***

***Dated: February 05, 2019***

**To,**

**The Members,**

**All Pakistan Marble Industries Association**

Subject: **FORMATION OF DELEGATION OF ALL PAKISTAN MARBLE INDUSTRIES ASSOCIATION (APMIA) FOR XIAMEN STONE FAIR, CHINA FROM 06-09 MARCH, 2019**

Respected Members,

This is to inform you that All Pakistan Marble Industries Association (APMIA) is planning to make a TDAP approved delegation of 10 to 15 members to visit Xiamen Stone Fair, China to be held from 06-09 March, 2019.

Interested members can contact APMIA Head Office along with the copy of their passport and ID card, so we can send their details for the approval.

Last date of submission is **13th February, 2019 (Wednesday)**

**(Note: This facility is only for APMIA members having valid membership, and it would be a self financed visit. All the members of the delegation will bare all of their expenses on their own including air ticketing, boarding, traveling, lodging etc)**

Thanking You.

**Chairman – Mian Gulzar Ahmad**

**BUSINESS VISA REQUIREMENTS FOR CHINA**

**Respected Members,**

Following are the business visa requirements to visit china to attend 18th China Xiamen International Stone Fair, China to be held from 06-09 March, 2019

- Three photographs with white background
- Copy of passport with six months validity
- Copy of China Visa (for those who already visited China)
- Copy of CNIC
- Copy of Local Chamber Certificate
- Copy of NTN Certirificate
- Copy of Membership Certificate of APMIA
- Copy of Income Tax Return
- Visa request on company letterhead to the Visa Officer
- Police verification in case of travelling first time to China and attested from ministry of Foreign Affairs
- Bank Statement of last six months.
- Processing fee Rs. 20,000 (Rupees twenty thousand only) in the shape of Bank Draft / Pay Order or Cheque in favor of All Pakistan Marble Industries Association.
- In case of applicant is not available or in case of rejection Rs. 10,000 (Rupees ten Thousand only) will be non-refundable.

**Last date of the submission is 13th February, 2019**
