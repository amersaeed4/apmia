A delegation of Chinese companies representing Mining & Processing Machinery visited our zonal office in Mardan, KP, to explore investment prospects and bilateral cooperation in Marble & Granite Mining and processing. Asghar Khan Chairman APMIA, accompanied by Wisal khan President MIA, and other zone leaders, extended a warm welcome, assuring complete cooperation. Additionally, APMIA enthusiastically invites all international investors to consider Pakistan for investment opportunities, promising unwavering support from our end.
