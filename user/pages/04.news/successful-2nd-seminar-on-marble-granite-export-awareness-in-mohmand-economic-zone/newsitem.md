A thriving seminar took place today at the Mohmand Economic Zone, marking the second event in a series focused on Marble & Granite Export Awareness. Distinguished speakers included:

Mr. Asghar Khan Utmankhel, Chairman of the All Pakistan Marble Industries Association  
Mr. Shakir Ullah, CEC Member of the All Pakistan Marble Industries Association  
Mr. Omar Hussan Bajwa, Assistant Director, Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP)  
Mr. Muhammad Bilal Khan, CEO of Mohmand Dada Minerals  
Mr. Mazhar Abbas Khan, CEO of Samawat International Corporation

Guest Speaker Engr. Said Muhammad, Vice Chairman of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Board of Investment & Trade (KPBOIT), added valuable insights to the seminar.

In recognition of their valuable contributions, shields and certificates were presented to the participants.

This seminar is just the beginning; the initiative will not stop here. Plans are already in motion to take this awareness and collaboration to other potential clusters in the near future
