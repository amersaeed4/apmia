***Ref No.: APMIA/HO/2019/2002***

**To,**

**The Members,**

**All Pakistan Marble Industries Association**

Subject: **FORMATION OF DELEGATION OF ALL PAKISTAN MARBLE INDUSTRIES ASSOCIATION (APMIA) FOR MARBLE IZMIR FAIR, TURKEY FROM 27-30 MARCH, 2019**

**Respected Members,**

This is to inform you that All Pakistan Marble Industries Association (APMIA) has planned to form a delegation of 10 to 15 members to visit Marble Izmir Fair, Turkey to be held from 27-30 March, 2019.

Interested members can contact APMIA Head Office along with the copy of their passport and ID card, so we can send their details for the approval.

Last date of submission is **18th February, 2019 (Monday)**

**(Note: This facility is only for APMIA members having valid membership, and it would be a self financed visit. All the members of the delegation will bare all of their expenses on their own including air ticketing, boarding, traveling, lodging etc)**

Thanking You

Syed Masud Gillani

Secretary General

All Pakistan Marble Industries Association

Subject**BUSINESS VISA REQUIREMENTS FOR TURKEY**

**Respected Members,**

Following are the business visa requirements to visit Turkey to attend Izmir Stone Fair in Izmir, Turkey to be held from 27-30 March, 2019

- Visa Application Form with two(2) photographs (White Background – 5x5cm), biometric (Visa form should have the same photo)
- Visa Interview Form (Given by Gerry’s office)
- Request Letter by Applicant
- Passport / Travel Document along with copies of the recent and old passports (If lost, Attested Police Report and it’s English Translation)
- Travel health insurance certificate
- Polio vaccination certificate
- Copy of the flight reservation / booking
- Copy of the hotel reservation (Reservation only) or invitation letter which mentions accommodation details if applicable
- Family registration certificate from NADRA office
- In case applicant is owner of a business a statement on a company letterhead paper about purpose of the visit
- FBR / NTN / Income / Active Tax Payer Certificate(s) for individual and company which are recently taken from the relevant Tax Offices
- Bank account maintenance certificates for personal and company accounts along with original bank statements of the last three (3) months
- Recommendation Letter and Membership Certificate from local Chamber of Commerce and Industry
- Recommendation Letter and Membership Certificate of related Trade Organization.
- Processing fee Rs. 30,000/- in the shape of Bank Draft or Cheque in favor of All Pakistan Marble Industries Association.
- In case of applicant is not available or in case of rejection Rs. 10,000 (Rupees ten Thousand only) will be non-refundable.

**Last date of the submission is 18th February, 2019 (Monday)**

Syed Masud Gillani

(Secretary General)
