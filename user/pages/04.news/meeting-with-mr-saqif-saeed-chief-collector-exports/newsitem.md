i) With reference to the meeting held by APMIA representatives with Mr. Saqif Saeed, Chief Collector, Exports, on 05 November, 2018, APMIA has received the written confirmation letter and they agreed the formula and accepted what we presented to them. We had received a complaint that all old export GDs which our exporters filed on less than 150 USD per ton were not completed by custom staff, I approached chief collector Manzoor Memon on last Friday to resolve our long standing issue. It’s resolved and all those pending GDs will be completed today.
