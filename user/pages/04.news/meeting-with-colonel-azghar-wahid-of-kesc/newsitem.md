Meeting of Mr. Muhammad Bilal Khan, Senior Vice Chairman-APMIA with Colonel Azghar Wahid of KESC, was held regarding the issues faced by the Marble Industries at Manghopir and site area, Karachi. They discussed the problems faced by our members and also demanded the eradication of those factors which impede our real growth. Assurance were given and very soon APMIA delegation will also meet the Chief Executive Officer of KESC for further detailed discussion which will help us to resolve the matters related to electricity.
